⚙ How to craft & farm runes

Horadric Cube recipe

  1. Get a white (Common) base item of the right type (chest, sword, staff…).
  2. Open the Horadric Cube and place the base + the exact runes inside.
  3. Press Transmute — button lights up when the combo is valid.

Notes

• Runes can go in any order; no sockets needed on the base.
• Blue items can be stripped to white via the Cube's Remove Affixes.
• The result is a Unique — you can socket, enchant, and masterwork it.
• Salvaging the runeword usually returns the runes.
• This bypasses the normal 2-rune limit — recipes use 2–5 runes.

Farming

• The Countess — dedicated rune boss, ~5 runes per kill.
• The Pit — higher tiers = better rune quality.
• Seasonal quest — early rewards include Tal + Eth (free Stealth).
• Helltides / Nightmare Dungeons elites; Purveyor gambling; trading; Rebirth transfers.

New runes this season

Evocation: Ber, Ist, Ith, Lo, Ral, Sol, Tir · Ritual: Amn, Eth, Mal, Ort, Tal · Older: Jah, Ohm, Vex, Thul. Ber & Jah are the rare chase runes.
